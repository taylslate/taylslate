## How to interpret the sources

- **Decisions:** a documented founder choice remains a choice until explicitly superseded. A newer file modification date alone does not override it.
- **Implementation:** code and commit history establish what is implemented locally. They do not prove deployment or production success.
- **Live verification:** use dated proof of the specific workflow. Card-on-file proof does not prove a charge or payout.
- **Evidence:** preserve source, date, scope, and limitations. A founder observation is useful evidence but is not a measured customer outcome.
- **Hypotheses:** positioning, retention predictions, forecasts, and competitor interpretations remain hypotheses unless supported by the appropriate evidence.
- **Conflict:** use a clearly documented supersession when one exists; otherwise flag the discrepancy. Do not silently choose a new strategy.
